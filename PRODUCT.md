# Product

## Product Name

Desic Terminal

## Register

product

## Users

Active crypto derivatives traders and analysts who use a desktop terminal for continuous market monitoring, chart analysis, order management, and AI-assisted research or execution.

## Product Purpose

Provide a fast, dependable OKX perpetual trading workspace where realtime market data, durable news and smart-money intelligence, local historical data, charting, account operations, and an AI trading assistant work as one coherent desktop tool.

Market Intelligence news is stored locally as durable article and event evidence. The news workspace supports local-day navigation, bounded pagination, and per-device read cursors; background refreshes count only articles first seen after the cursor, so repeated synchronization does not create false unread alerts.

The Current Orders and Historical Orders workspaces group orders by exchange semantics rather than by a generic ordinary/strategy split: limit/market, advanced limit, take-profit/stop-loss, trailing protection, and trigger orders each have a dedicated tab. Iceberg, TWAP, or unknown algo types remain visible in an additional tab when present. Current categories retain their native amend, cancel, status, and price-field behavior; historical categories combine durable ordinary-order history with the separately synchronized algo-order history without inventing missing price or PnL fields.

AI position management treats stop-loss protection and take-profit exit as separate decisions. Existing-position take-profit uses an explicit close opportunity with `exitKind=take_profit` and a reduce-only limit or market exit; stop-loss uses `exitKind=stop_loss` with a trigger or market exit. A run may leave the target empty when no evidence-based target exists, but it must state that decision and the next reevaluation condition rather than silently treating a stop as a complete exit plan. Before creating a new exit, AI reconciles exact order identities and semantic roles; changing an existing target or stop uses an amend opportunity on that order, while multiple exits are reserved for explicitly documented, non-overlapping staged quantities.

## Current Market Radar Boundary

Market Radar is the independent cross-market discovery and ranking workspace for live OKX USDT perpetuals. It does not depend on watchlist membership: the product directory comes from OKX instruments data, while a bounded all-tickers snapshot supplies current price, 24-hour change, turnover, spread, and lightweight activity evidence. Stock-category instruments are enriched with searchable security names and listing exchanges from the official NASDAQ Trader Symbol Directory. Chinese interfaces additionally resolve Simplified or Traditional Chinese entity labels from Wikidata by exact ticker and listing exchange; localized matches are cached independently, refreshed as new instruments appear, and fall back through the official English name to the OKX contract code. The desktop refreshes the market snapshot adaptively rather than subscribing every contract to high-frequency ticker, trade, book, or candle streams. Opening a chart and adding a contract to the watchlist remain separate actions.

Market Radar prepares a layered local research baseline without extending watchlist-grade 1-minute storage to the full market. Live USDT perpetuals receive up to 400 days of UTC-boundary `1Dutc` history; the top 100 contracts by current USDT turnover additionally receive 90 days of `1H` history. Stock perpetual return research uses a separately identified forward-adjusted daily series. Built-in ranking combines explainable daily relative strength, realized volatility, turnover change, and trend stability with a smaller current-snapshot component. Data coverage, freshness, and fallback to snapshot-only scoring remain visible. Rankings prioritize research; they are not profit forecasts, trading advice, strategy signals, or order commands. The advanced factor expression workbench is an expert research layer and is not a primary Strategy Research tab.

AI Research keeps the center-panel research and market-intelligence shortcuts in a small vertical rail on the panel's right edge; selecting either shortcut opens the right inspector even when it is closed. The desktop retains one replaceable research snapshot per UTC hour for 90 days. These snapshots provide 1-hour, 1-day, and 7-day rank changes, weighted score-change attribution, deterministic saved filters, transition-based research alerts, and a shared point-in-time record for comparison and validation. Alerts are deduplicated, cooled down, and capped; they only publish research notifications and cannot place orders. Historical validation evaluates saved point-in-time universes against later confirmed daily candles at 1, 5, and 20 days, with Rank IC, top/bottom quantile spreads, a saved-spread cost proxy, turnover, chronological training/validation segments, and BTC-defined market regimes. It never reconstructs prior universes from today's surviving instruments. Natural-language filtering is compiled into visible deterministic clauses; unsupported text remains visible instead of being used as an opaque market decision.

AI can inspect this persisted research state through the built-in `market-radar-research` Skill and read-only `radar.*` tools. Broad current-market analysis or market-overview requests without one named instrument route first through persisted breadth and default composite ranking; real-time claims add live evidence and remain timestamp-distinct from the hourly Radar snapshot. The tool boundary exposes ranking, one-instrument evidence, 2-4 market comparison, category breadth, rank history, point-in-time validation, and saved deterministic filter definitions. Results always retain snapshot time, model version, universe coverage, ranking basis, global rank versus scoped rank, and limitations; they read SQLite directly and never infer state from the current UI tab. Radar AI tools cannot write snapshots, save filters, change alerts, create strategies or opportunities, submit orders, or modify account state.

## Current Systematic Research Boundary

Systematic Research is a local, single-contract strategy research workspace for eligible USDT perpetuals. Its scheduler invokes Python `on_bar` only after a confirmed 1-minute K-line, while strategy code can read current-time-bounded `1m`, `3m`, `5m`, `15m`, `30m`, `1H`, `2H`, `4H`, `6H`, `12H`, and `1D` series. A higher-timeframe final bar may be explicitly marked incomplete and contains only information available at that minute. New strategy creation requires an explicit template choice: a minimal no-trade blank entrypoint or a bundled research starter; the create action no longer silently inserts trading logic. Users can version Python packages with immutable saved `ctx.params`, current virtual-account state, open orders, fills, closed trades, and funding records. A save creates a new immutable version only when the name, description, source, parameters, or desktop-owned tuning ranges changed. The version history supports paged inspection and comparison, can load a historical snapshot only into the current unsaved draft, and can start a new backtest or Profile from an exact historical version. A package returns one high-level market or limit opening/closing action, an order cancellation, a no-action, or a position-protection action; it cannot access credentials, query arbitrary data, or submit an exchange order. Desktop-owned numeric parameter ranges are editable only in the desktop tuning workbench, are fixed when a run starts, and cannot be declared by source code.

### Parameter tuning workbench

Parameter tuning uses a dedicated workbench instead of exposing the optimization action beside raw JSON. The workbench recommends signal parameters, keeps protection and sizing parameters opt-in, validates the current value against each range, and offers fixed Fast/Standard/Deep budgets of 30, 100, or 300 candidate groups. Starting a run saves the current draft as an immutable research version, pins that version to the run, uses the fixed 70/30 train-validation split, and samples oversized grids deterministically so the same version and ranges reproduce the same candidate set. The optimizer reuses loaded K-line and contract data, keeps one Python process per CPU-aware worker lane, and reports progress, elapsed/estimated remaining time, and cancellation state. Completed results include the baseline validation Calmar, sampling mode, candidate budget, and the saved strategy version; adopting a result immediately creates the next strategy version and opens its backtest settings.

Backtests pin the Python strategy version, local K-line snapshot, virtual initial equity, preloaded confirmed history before the formal evaluation start, leverage (1x-50x), safety-margin multiplier, conservative fees/slippage, and end-of-run policy. Formal evaluation defaults to the latest 30 days ending at least one hour before the current time, and supports a maximum 366-day one-minute range; preload remains visible to the first decision but never enters equity, replay, PnL, drawdown, or statistics. Market actions fill at the next minute open. Limit actions enter a pending-order state at that open and then use only later 1-minute OHLCV traversal plus a volume-participation cap, so their results are explicitly a conservative K-line estimate rather than an order-book-queue simulation. Pending limits can partially fill, remain open, be cancelled, or expire. A full simulated close clears protection only after it fully fills; a partial close retains it. When remaining virtual margin or the configured entry budget cannot reach the contract minimum, the backtest records an opening `no_action` with the sizing reason and continues instead of failing the whole run. The virtual-margin exhaustion rule is deliberately conservative and is not an exchange liquidation-price estimate. Results preserve a complete local ledger and candle-by-candle replay with fill labels, point-in-time virtual balance and margin, current/historical positions, reproducible statistics, total elapsed time, and persisted phase diagnostics. Long replays initially load a bounded recent candle page; moving the replay timeline loads the page containing the selected time while preserving the account and ledger state. Backtest records expose the exact pinned strategy version. The stateful backtest contract rejects look-ahead data and implicit position reversals. Transparent K-line factors remain research evidence, not trained-model validation or trade commands.

In Strategy Research, the desktop app ships a bundled, checksum-verified CPython 3.13 runtime inside its resources and creates one Desic-owned local virtual environment from it when needed; development builds without a staged bundle fall back to a compatible Python 3.12-3.13 interpreter on PATH. That environment installs only the fixed scientific dependency set used by the source-policy allowlist and runs a strategy through the current-time JSONL contract. Python receives no account credentials, proxy configuration, arbitrary data API, or order function. The local venv is dependency isolation rather than an operating-system security sandbox, so Profiles must be activated only for source the user has reviewed and trusts.

An enabled Strategy Profile pins an exact Python version, account, environment, contract, cross/isolated margin mode, target leverage, direction permissions, per-entry margin budget, same-side total margin budget, daily realized-loss limit, cooldown, and notification preference. It reuses the existing confirmed 1-minute K-line stream and fresh private-account snapshot; it does not create a second market subscription. Before evaluation, the host waits briefly for the just-closed candle to settle in local storage, repairs the complete visible history plus the recent tail when necessary, and verifies the exact confirmed cutoff again. A failed repair skips that cycle and records the real synchronization diagnostic; it never evaluates against a partial window and retries on later confirmed closes. The Python process returns only a validated open/close intent with a reason; the desktop converts eligible opens into exchange-valid contract counts from the Profile budget, instrument contract value, minimum size, lot size, equity, leverage, and existing same-side usage. It then applies Profile risk checks and sends eligible market or limit orders through the existing audited, idempotent WSS-first order route. Before entries, the host checks and, if required, synchronizes actual OKX leverage to the Profile target. Entry-attached TP/SL is supported. Profile settings expose market or trigger-after-limit execution for protection sides statically declared by the pinned strategy source; take-profit also supports a post-fill resting limit submitted immediately after actual entry fills and resized as partial fills arrive. The host never invents a missing stop-loss or take-profit. An accepted close request is only recorded as submitted; Desic does not manually cancel attached TP/SL on that acknowledgement, and OKX cancels exchange-managed attached TP/SL once the position is fully flat. Before a Profile strategy close is submitted, the host cancels that Profile's post-fill resting TP orders and marks them superseded so reconciliation cannot recreate them. Dynamic protection amendment remains disabled for live Profiles until reconciliation is complete. When an OKX account is saved or tested, the desktop verifies its position mode and automatically switches `net_mode` to `long_short_mode` when the API key can write account settings. If OKX refuses the switch because positions or orders remain, the notification center explains how to flatten and retry, and activation stays blocked. Live activation requires an exact completed backtest, local Python availability, account read/trade permission, hedge mode, conflict review against active AI automation, and an explicit confirmation. Profiles cannot be edited while running. Signal history preserves actions, blocks, order identifiers, and errors; a per-cycle runtime, snapshot, or execution error is recorded for review instead of immediately stopping the Profile. Optional Feishu delivery also obeys the global strategy-signal event setting.

The three interactive AI assistants use one open Cline Agent runtime. Their conversations remain scope-bound and persisted locally, including bounded text, reasoning, and process checkpoints while a turn is streaming, so switching sessions or restarting the app retains the visible partial result and marks an interrupted turn explicitly. Open-agent mode (enabled by default) also exposes Cline's native file, shell, browser, network, MCP, and user Skill capabilities. Users can choose workspace roots and use their own Skills or MCP servers. External files, web pages, and media are research input and cannot change Desic's system role or trading controls. Strategy edits remain versioned and tested, indicators remain safe DSL documents, and account or market operations remain structured Desic tool calls. Direct order submission, Profile activation, credential access, and exchange reconciliation stay behind the existing Desic authorization and audit chain; opening the Agent runtime does not inject API secrets into Cline.

AI Research is the default top-level workspace for user-initiated research conversations. It replaces the floating assistant rather than overlaying another workspace, keeps its controller mounted while users navigate elsewhere, and retains the shared market/account header and every existing workspace. Its desktop surface is a resizable session sidebar, research stream, and optional resizable research inspector. The inspector collapses on narrow layouts, keeps multiple closeable tabs, and can pin a market reading, Skill detail, strategy result, account evidence, trade precheck, or intelligence result without turning a permanent blank sidebar into dead space. Its center-edge rail also opens a compact Market Radar preview with important ranking tabs. Strategy tool results render returned Python source as a readable code block, and strategy tabs render the same source in a read-only editor surface while explicitly routing continuing edits to Strategy Research. Market Radar remains an independent discovery workspace and is read through attributable `radar.*` evidence instead of being duplicated inside AI Research. Tool, reasoning, sub-agent, approval, and failure states use compact domain-specific process rows; aligned evidence fields are primary and raw payloads are secondary disclosure. The composer exposes common research starters and enabled Skills through one `/` palette. Its context trigger shows the latest Cline message occupancy and a catalog capacity, configured fallback, or default 256K capacity; the displayed percentage is `latest assistant inputTokens ÷ contextWindow`, while the system/tool/conversation composition is a separate local `text length ÷ 4` estimate rather than Provider-billed usage. Cline's native basic compaction pipeline preserves the complete Desic audit transcript while compacting only the next provider request at 90% of the effective context window. While a turn is active, Enter uses Cline's native queue and Cmd/Ctrl+Enter uses native steer; pending prompts remain editable and removable, and Stop cancels both the active turn and pending prompts. User sessions may receive one bounded asynchronous model-generated title after their first question, with a deterministic fallback and no title generation for automation, strategy, or indicator sessions. Markdown, drafts, scroll position, unread output, measured timing, and current Cline-message context usage remain visible without inventing unavailable Provider data. Branching from a completed message is non-destructive: it copies retained conversation history into a new Cline continuation, leaves the source session unchanged, and does not roll back files, tools, trades, or any other prior side effect.

AI Research keeps inspector tabs isolated per conversation. Completed market tools open a dedicated market-data workspace with localized human-readable labels, returned metrics, and factual charts; `market.readCandles` renders the returned OHLC sequence as a K-line chart, while indicator and order-book payloads render their actual series and depth structure. The default market tab surfaces the selected pair's live ticker, K-line stream, order-book pressure, funding, and cached sentiment when available. The Market Intelligence section is a compact right-rail preview with icon-only section navigation, horizontal five-view tabs (news/events, sentiment, derivatives, Smart Money, and history), full scrollable evidence lists, a complete calendar table, intuitive liquidation and system-risk summaries with supporting samples, and an icon action to open the full Market Intelligence workspace. News uses the local feed path for fast first paint and all intelligence views preserve freshness/source caveats; raw transport fields remain behind data-details disclosure. Session history places user-pinned sessions first, exposes rename/delete actions on hover or focus, and shows a live running indicator without changing persisted trading or approval semantics.

Agent collaboration templates carry bounded reusable instructions and collaboration roles. Profile Skills, model, reasoning depth, account, environment, up to three watched symbols, and limits remain Profile-owned. The former template-level phase, Skills, model, and reasoning fields are compatibility-only and have no current editor/runtime effect. Templates remain configuration only: applying one never changes a Profile's permission mode, account binding, risk limits, or fixed tool safety boundaries, and template text is appended after the fixed policy rules rather than replacing them. Custom Agents do not require users to configure data scopes; an unscoped custom Agent inherits the Profile-permitted delegated read and precheck tools. Queued Runs retain a frozen template instruction snapshot for reproducibility. For local Codex CLI delegation, the selected Desic reasoning tier is applied as an explicit per-thread configuration override while the official CLI retains ownership of its existing OAuth, API-key, or OS-keyring credential store; Desic does not read, copy, or parse `auth.json`.

Skills can additionally be installed as immutable resource bundles. A user must explicitly grant execution trust before the interactive main Agent can invoke a named Node or Python entrypoint through the host-controlled `skill.run` tool. Bundle execution verifies the content hash, uses a fixed packaged runtime and locked dependency environment, exchanges bounded JSON only, and is unavailable to delegated Agents and background Profiles. This dependency isolation is not an operating-system sandbox; users must review a bundle before trusting it.

For a concrete USDT linear-perpetual candidate, deterministic plan evaluation and the final exchange/account precheck distinguish gross target profit from fee-adjusted net profit. They expose the fee source, break-even price, target fee drag, fee-inclusive reward/risk, and returns relative to margin and equity while explicitly excluding unmodeled slippage and funding. Fixed-size leverage changes estimated margin and margin-relative percentages, not absolute fees or price PnL; the AI must not use higher leverage or a wider technical stop to make a weak net opportunity appear attractive. Once an entry thesis is valid without a future crossing trigger, the AI prefers a reasonably fillable resting ordinary limit order for potential maker-fee savings, while recognizing that marketable limits can still be taker orders and resting limits can miss or face adverse selection.

The AI strategy assistant remains bound to the currently selected Python strategy editor and may read, test, and update only that unsaved editor buffer through revision-checked tools. The AI trading assistant uses a separate session-scoped strategy research workspace: it may create strategies, save immutable versions, backtest, compare, and optimize strategies created in that same conversation, but it cannot read or modify an existing strategy editor or activate a Strategy Profile. Research results can be opened in Strategy Research without replacing the current editor draft.

AI Token usage is based on Provider or official CLI usage reports rather than local text-length estimates. A turn includes every model iteration plus each reported sub-Agent exactly once; cache read/write is an input breakdown rather than an additional total. Run details show cache hit rate only when both input and cache-read coverage were reported, using cache-read tokens divided by total input tokens. Missing or partial Provider usage remains visibly incomplete instead of being represented as zero, and historical local usage can be rebuilt from retained raw usage events.

Every background trading Profile reconciles watched positions with current ordinary and algo exit orders before creating new exposure. An unprotected position requires an explicit hold-and-exit condition, a protective close opportunity, or a justified reduction/close. Opposite long/short positions remain allowed as deliberate hedges only with a stated objective, size relationship, duration, unwind condition, exits, and combined portfolio risk. New Profiles use a 30-minute maximum-silence fallback by default; saved user values remain unchanged.

Strategy Profile runtime, market-data, account-snapshot, and host position-sizing failures are tracked consecutively. After three consecutive runtime failures, the affected Profile is disabled and the notification center records the automatic stop. Ordinary risk blocks such as cooldown, direction permission, or daily loss limits are audited but do not count as runtime failures.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, professional. The interface should feel focused under sustained use and communicate risk, state, and execution outcomes without decorative noise.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing-page composition, oversized headings, decorative cards, excessive rounding, bright ornamental gradients, and interactions that cover critical trading data without a clear reason.

## Design Principles

- Keep realtime trading information scannable and stable during rapid updates.
- Make state, permission, risk, and execution consequences explicit.
- Preserve workspace context while revealing secondary tools progressively.
- Treat news, macro events, sentiment, and smart-money activity as attributable evidence, never as an implicit trade command.
- Prefer familiar desktop-terminal interactions over novel controls.
- Keep dense workflows efficient without making the interface visually loud.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable contrast, keyboard-operable controls, non-color status cues, and reduced-motion fallbacks for every transition.
The system interface follows the operating-system language by default and supports explicit language selection, while preserving Prompt, Skill, AI-generated, user-authored, and third-party original content verbatim.
