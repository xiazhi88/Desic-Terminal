## Design Context

### Users

Active crypto derivatives traders and analysts who keep the desktop terminal open for sustained market monitoring, chart analysis, order management, and AI-assisted research or execution. The interface must support fast scanning and repeated actions under changing market conditions.

### Brand Personality

Precise, calm, professional. Desic Terminal should feel decisive and technically capable without becoming noisy, playful, or decorative.

### Aesthetic Direction

A high-contrast trading command deck built on near-black surfaces, restrained purple brand accents, conventional financial red/green semantics, condensed display typography for primary market data, and compact desktop-terminal controls. Avoid marketing layouts, ornamental gradients, excessive glow, oversized rounding, and decoration that competes with live trading information.

### Design Principles

- Concentrate visual impact on the current market, live price, chart, order-book depth, and trading actions.
- Keep dense information stable and scannable during rapid updates; never animate layout dimensions.
- Use purple for brand, focus, active state, and limited structural emphasis rather than broad decoration.
- Preserve all critical trading context and keep secondary tools progressively available without covering data.
- Respect keyboard operation, readable contrast, non-color status cues, and reduced-motion preferences.
